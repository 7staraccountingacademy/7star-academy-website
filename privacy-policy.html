Privacy Policy

How we collect and use student information to provide recorded courses, quizzes, payments and certificates.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Who we are

7 STAR Accounting Academy ("Academy", "we", "our") provides recorded accounting courses, online lessons, practical quizzes, study materials, examinations and related learner support through our website at https://7staraccountingacademy.github.io/7star-academy-website/.

2. Information we collect

  • Registration: your name, email address, mobile number and city.
  • Account and learning: authentication/account identifiers, course access status, lessons completed, practical quiz marks and related activity.
  • Payment records: payment status and transaction/order reference details needed to confirm enrolment or resolve issues. We do not ask you to send card numbers, UPI PINs or OTPs.
  • Final exam and certificate: the name, email address, answers, scores and certificate information you provide when using the examination process.
  • Technical information: necessary browser, security or diagnostic information that the website or third-party hosting, authentication or embedded-service providers may process.

3. How we use information

  • Create and authenticate your student account; confirm eligibility and course payment.
  • Deliver course videos, eBooks, tests and learning progress; display your own dashboard.
  • Assess examinations, communicate results and issue certificates when eligible.
  • Provide technical support, prevent misuse and investigate payment or access problems.
  • Keep records where required for accounting, disputes or other applicable legal obligations.

4. Services involved

We use third-party services for parts of the learning experience, including Firebase/Google for account and progress functionality, Razorpay for payment processing, YouTube and Google Drive for educational content, Google Forms/Sheets or associated Google services for examination and certificate workflows, and GitHub Pages for website hosting. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Following external links may take you to their platforms.

5. Sharing and security

We do not sell student personal information. We share or allow processing of data only as reasonably needed to run the course, use necessary service providers, provide support or meet applicable legal requirements. We apply appropriate access controls, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Storage and retention

We retain account, learning, payment and certificate records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the course, maintain records, handle disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. Data may be stored or processed through our service providers in locations where they operate.

7. Your choices and requests

You may ask us to review or correct inaccurate account information, request information about your data, or request deletion where applicable. We may need to verify your identity and may need to retain certain records for legitimate or legal purposes. Deletion of necessary account data may make continued course access unavailable.

8. Children and external content

Students below 18 should use the service with permission and guidance from a parent or legal guardian. Our website contains links to educational and social platforms not controlled by us; review their privacy policies when you use them.

9. Updates

We may update this policy as our platform, service providers or legal requirements change. The current version and revision date will appear on this page. Material changes will be communicated in a suitable manner when required.
