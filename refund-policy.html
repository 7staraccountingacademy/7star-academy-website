Refund & Cancellation Policy

Our normal no-refund policy, and what to do if a payment is duplicated or course access does not work.

Last updated: 26 November 2026
Our standard course policy: The ₹99 online recorded course is a digital service. Once a purchase is successfully completed and course access has been granted, requests to cancel or obtain a refund solely because of a change of mind are normally not accepted. This does not remove any rights available under applicable law.

1. Digital course and cancellation

There is no physical shipment or return. After payment verification, access to the recorded course is provided through your student account. Once access is granted, the ordinary cancellation/return facility is not offered for a change of mind, lack of time or personal preference.

2. When a payment issue can be reviewed

  • Duplicate payment: if the same course has been charged more than once by mistake.
  • Payment deducted but access missing: if successful payment cannot be matched to your student account and the issue is not resolved after reasonable investigation.
  • Significant service non-delivery: if the paid digital course cannot be provided or reasonably restored.
  • Other legally required cases: whenever a refund, correction or other remedy is required under applicable consumer or payment law.

We will first investigate and, where appropriate, correct payment status or restore access. If a refund is appropriate or legally required, it will be processed to the original payment method through the payment provider, where supported.

3. How to report a problem

Contact the Academy through the official WhatsApp Business link below. Please provide your registered email address, approximate date and amount of payment, transaction/order reference, and a short description or screenshot of the issue. Never provide OTPs, account passwords, card PINs, UPI PINs or complete card information.

If money was debited and your course is still locked, please do not pay a second time before contacting us.

4. Review and refund timing

We will review reported problems in a reasonable time. Where a refund is approved or required, the time for the credit to appear depends on the payment method, bank and payment gateway processing. Any applicable legal timelines take precedence. We will communicate the result using the contact information provided.

5. Student rights

This policy does not restrict valid consumer complaints, statutory remedies, or rights under payment network and applicable law. We do not charge a separate cancellation fee.
