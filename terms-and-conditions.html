At the currently advertised ₹99 offer, the platform provides 21 chapter-wise video lessons, five practical questions per lesson (105 in total), a separate approximately three-hour complete-course video, available study materials, a 30-question final examination, and a certificate process for eligible learners. Content and availability are as described on the website at purchase.

4. Practical tests, examination and certificate

Practical lesson quizzes help you track progress. A lesson is marked completed after a score of at least 3 out of 5 answers on its practical test. The separate 30-question final examination has a passing requirement of 18 out of 30 marks. When the examination and certificate conditions are met, the PDF certificate is sent through the existing certificate process to the email submitted for the examination. Use your registered email to help prevent delivery issues.

Certificates acknowledge completion of our Academy's training assessment. They are not represented as a government-issued qualification, professional licence or guarantee of employment.
